# Acme Corp – HR Policy Handbook (Demo)

## 1. Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive **24 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried forward to the next year; carried days expire on 31 March.
- Leave requests of 3+ days must be submitted at least **2 weeks** in advance through the HR portal.

## 2. Sick Leave
- **12 days** of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for absences longer than **2 consecutive days**.

## 3. Work From Home
- Employees may work from home up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 local time.

## 4. Travel & Expenses
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed above 6 hours.
- Daily meal allowance: **USD 50** domestic, **USD 75** international.
- Expense claims must be submitted within **30 days** with receipts.

## 5. Parental Leave
- Primary caregivers: **26 weeks** fully paid.
- Secondary caregivers: **6 weeks** fully paid, to be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption.

## 6. Notice Period
- During probation (first 3 months): **2 weeks**.
- After probation: **1 month**; managers and above: **2 months**.
